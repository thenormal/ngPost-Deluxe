# Guided Post

Guided Post asks a few questions and prepares the post for you. It is the first entry of the left bar. It is an extra way to post: [Quick Post](04-quick-post.md) and [Auto Posting](08-auto-posting.md) stay as they are, and they do the actual work, with the same checks.

## When to use it

If you post for the first time, or you do not remember which page to use, start here. If you know what you want, you can still use it to prepare several posts and start them together.

## The five steps

1. **What do you want to do?** Post the chosen files as **one release**, post **each file or folder** as its own release, **scan a folder once**, or **watch a folder** and post what arrives.
2. **What to post.** Add files and folders (drag them into the window, or use the buttons; double click an item to remove it). For a scan or a watch, choose the folder.
3. **Options.** Choose a profile (the **Recommended** one is suggested) or press **Customize** to change compression, random name and password, par2, the NFO file and, for the Auto Posting choices, what happens to the files after posting.
4. **Where to post.** Shows your servers (with the result of the last test), your newsgroups and the folder of the NZB files. The buttons open Servers or Settings to change them. Check **Skip this step when everything is set** to see it only when something is missing.
5. **Summary.** Shows what will be posted and the checks, in plain words, with the same fixes as the other pages. You can save your choices as a profile with **Save these choices as a profile**. Press **Post** (or **Start watching**).

## Several posts at once

Press **Add another post** in the summary to prepare one more post: the ones already prepared are listed in **Posts ready**, where you can edit or remove them. The button becomes **Post all (n)**.

## Good to know

- One release opens a new tab in Quick Post; the other choices go to Auto Posting, where you follow them.
- The program remembers your last choices (not the files).
- Items already in the history are reported as usual before they are posted again.
- A folder can only be watched with compression.
